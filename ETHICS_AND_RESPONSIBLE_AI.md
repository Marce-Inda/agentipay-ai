# Responsible AI & Ethics Framework Specification 🤝🧠

At **AgenticPay AI**, ethical artificial intelligence principles are embedded into the core product lifecycle. Delegating financial authority to autonomous AI agents requires strict alignment with global AI ethics standards, ensuring **Human Autonomy, Non-Discrimination, Explainability, and Algorithmic Fairness**.

---

## 🏛️ Alignment with Global AI Ethics Frameworks

### 1. UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence
* **Human Agency & Oversight:** The AI agent acts exclusively as an *instrument of human intent*. It cannot initiate financial actions outside pre-approved budget boundaries or without explicit user delegation.
* **Transparency & Explicability:** The AG Grid command center presents a real-time **Thought Chain Matrix**, rendering the exact logical rationale, confidence score, and verification evidence behind every monetary action.
* **Environmental Sustainability:** Efficient prompt design and model selection (using light, high-performance models like Gemini Flash) minimize carbon footprint per transaction.

---

### 2. EU AI Act (Regulation EU 2024/1689 - High-Risk & Transparency Alignment)
* **Article 14 - Human Oversight (HITL / HOTL):**
  * *Human-in-the-Loop (HITL):* High-value transactions (> $50 USD) require mandatory One-Tap user confirmation.
  * *Human-on-the-Loop (HOTL):* Real-time emergency Kill-Switch allows users to intervene or revoke PayPal tokens instantaneously.
* **Article 15 - Accuracy, Robustness & Cybersecurity:**
  * Automated fallback: If AI confidence falls below 85%, the transaction is frozen and routed to human review, preventing erroneous automated payouts.

---

### 3. OECD AI Principles (OECD 2019/2024 Principles)
* **Fairness & Non-Discrimination:** In Agent-to-Agent (A2A) negotiations, prompts enforce fair market pricing bounds based on objective data, prohibiting predatory or exploitative bargaining strategies.
* **Accountability:** Every automated transaction generates an immutable audit hash connecting the user's initial prompt, the AI reasoning log, and the final PayPal Sandbox transaction receipt.
